03 / Method and coverage

What the numbers measure

Monthly market

The site reads published citywide and borough aggregates. Asking and effective rent are separate medians. Concession rate is the share of listings with concessions. The February 2025 to May 2026 series describes the project’s listing sample.

Neighborhood coverage

The May ranking uses the project’s supplied affordability, rent momentum, inventory momentum and pricing power scores. Staten Island is in market comparisons and has no scored neighborhoods. Its May borough snapshot contains 20 listings.

Reproduce the report

Data source is FirstMover NYC Open Data, as attributed in the project. The GitHub package includes the Power BI report, source CSVs and refresh instructions. The web lab uses aggregate tables and renders them in your browser.
