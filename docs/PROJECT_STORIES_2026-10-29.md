# Plain-language project walkthroughs

The ATLAS and E-Commerce previews previously relied on outlining technical
boxes. This update makes their motion more visible and explains the work in
four short chapters for readers who do not know the implementation tools.

## ATLAS

Sixteen moving packets travel along the existing schematic conduits. Small
meters and an active node guide the eye through the nine pipeline boxes.
Four plain-language chapters cover records, fair comparison, disclosed limits
and inspectable software. The drawing remains conceptual; no ATLAS dataset,
forecast, chart or dashboard is published. Failed calibration, hypothetical
planning and the held publication gate remain visible in the account.

## E-Commerce

A compact report replaces the dimensional-model drawing. It shows the
README's rounded revenue, order and customer figures, and its two delivery
percentages with their different denominators. Three category labels and
rounded amounts come from the original export, as already transcribed in the
analytics lab's pure model module. That module supplies the preview values;
tests also check the committed no-JavaScript HTML against it.

Bars reveal proportionally, then a moving highlight passes through each
filled bar. The numbers stay fixed. This is an animated walkthrough of
reported figures, not a live business feed. Currency is explicitly Brazilian
reais (R$); no monthly series or transaction records are invented.

## Controls and verification

Pause and Replay remain. Next step advances one chapter and pauses so a
visitor can read at their own pace, including with the keyboard. Reduced
motion restores the static overview, hides the ATLAS packets and shows the
complete reported bars. Without JavaScript, the overview, figures and source
captions remain readable. Offscreen, hidden-tab and filtered-card suspension
are retained. Larger previews scroll naturally on shorter desktop windows.

Unit tests check conduit bounds, deterministic frames, analytics provenance,
static markup and the delivery denominator. Browser tests check actual
packet/bar/highlight movement, pause behavior, keyboard chapters, fixed
labels, reduced motion and the existing desktop/phone layouts in both themes.

The page uses one additional same-origin import of the existing analytics
model. No runtime dependency or third-party service is added. The pathfinding
and synthetic security replays retain their existing engines and behavior.
